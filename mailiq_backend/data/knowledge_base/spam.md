# Spam Emails

Spam emails are unsolicited messages sent in bulk, often with deceptive subject lines and fake sender addresses. They include phishing attempts, lottery scams, fake package delivery notices, and fraudulent investment schemes.

Never click links in suspected spam emails. Links often lead to credential-harvesting pages or malware downloads. If you need to verify a claim from a company, navigate to their website directly.

Urgency language ("Act now", "Your account will be suspended", "You've won a prize") combined with requests for personal information, payment, or login credentials are the clearest spam signals.

Legitimate companies never ask for your password, full credit card number, or OTP via email. Any email requesting these is fraudulent regardless of how official it looks.

Phishing emails often spoof trusted domains — check the actual sender email address (not just the display name) to identify impersonation attempts.
