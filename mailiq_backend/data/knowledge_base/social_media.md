# Social Media Emails

Social media notification emails come from platforms like Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, or TikTok. They alert users about likes, comments, new followers, mentions, and platform-generated recommendations.

These emails are typically very low priority. Most are engagement-bait designed to pull you back to the app. They rarely contain information that can't be found inside the platform itself.

LinkedIn notifications are the exception — direct messages, connection requests, and InMail often require a professional response and may be more time-sensitive.

Social media account security emails (login from new device, password changed, 2FA request) are HIGH priority despite coming from social platforms — treat them like verify_code category emails.
