# Finance quick notes

Bank/UPI/card notifications are usually just confirmations, not action
items -- a debit or credit alert only needs attention if the amount or
merchant is unrecognized. EMI and bill-due reminders are the ones that
actually need action before a specific date.

Worth flagging fast: any "auto-debit failed" or "insufficient balance"
message, since these compound into late fees if missed for more than a
day or two.
