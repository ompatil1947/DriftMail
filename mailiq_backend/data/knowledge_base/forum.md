# Forum Emails

Forum notification emails are generated by platforms like Reddit, Stack Overflow, GitHub Discussions, or community forums. They notify users about replies, mentions, upvotes, and thread activity.

These emails are typically low priority unless they contain direct mentions or replies to your own posts. Most are informational digests that can be batched and reviewed later.

Thread hijacking and off-topic replies are common in forum digests — skim the subject line to identify which thread the email is about before reading the full body.

Watch for moderation notices (post removed, account warning) in forum emails — these require immediate attention and often have action deadlines.
