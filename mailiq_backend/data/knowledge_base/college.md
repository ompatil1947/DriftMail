# College quick notes

Institutional email ranges from truly time-sensitive (deadline extensions,
fee due dates, exam schedules) to informational (results, general
announcements). The subject line alone often undersells urgency -- "Fee
payment reminder" and "Assignment deadline extended" look similarly
routine but carry very different time pressure.

Faculty/advisor meeting requests usually expect a confirmation reply, not
just an acknowledgment -- worth surfacing that distinction rather than
treating all college mail as read-only announcements.
