# Promotions Emails

Promotional emails are marketing messages from brands, e-commerce platforms, subscription services, and newsletters. They typically advertise sales, discounts, new products, or special events.

These emails are almost always low priority and safe to batch-process or auto-archive. Urgency signals like "limited time offer" or "expires tonight" are marketing tactics and should not be taken at face value.

Legitimate promotional emails always include an unsubscribe link (required by law in most jurisdictions). Emails claiming to be promotional but lacking an unsubscribe link may be spam instead.

Look for promo codes embedded in the email body — these are often the only actionable content in an otherwise verbose promotional message.
