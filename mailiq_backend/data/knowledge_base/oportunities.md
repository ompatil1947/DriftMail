# Opportunities quick notes

Internship, hackathon, and job emails often bury the one deadline that
actually matters in a wall of formatting. Worth extracting: the exact
apply-by date, whether it's a rolling or fixed deadline, and whether any
action is needed now (confirm a slot, complete a profile) versus later.

For hackathon invites specifically, registration deadlines are usually
earlier than the event itself -- don't let "starts in 3 weeks" read as
low-urgency if registration actually closes in 3 days.
